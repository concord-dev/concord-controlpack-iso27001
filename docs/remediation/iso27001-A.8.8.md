# No CRITICAL or HIGH vulnerabilities are unresolved beyond their SLA

`ISO27001-A.8.8-vulnerability-management` · framework **iso27001** · severity **critical** · Technological Controls

## What this control checks

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.8.8 requires information about technical
vulnerabilities to be obtained, evaluated, and acted upon. Concord
reads the latest Snyk + Inspector scan report and verifies no
CRITICAL findings are older than the configured SLA.

## Why it matters

Vulnerability backlog is one of the most-investigated A.8.8 audit
artefacts. The SLA-based check ties policy to operational reality
rather than just count.

## Evidence

Collected from the `snyk` source (`org_issues` evidence type).

## What a failure looks like

This control reports a finding when any of the following hold:

- no vulnerability-scan evidence collected
- CRITICAL vuln <value> is <value> days old (SLA <value>) — <value>
- HIGH vuln <value> is <value> days old (SLA <value>) — <value>
- HIGH vuln <value> approaching SLA (<value>/<value> days) — <value>

## Remediation

Bring each affected resource or attestation listed under *What a failure looks like* into compliance, then re-collect evidence. Estimated effort: **1w**. Automated fix available: **false**.

## How to re-verify

```
concord check --controls <pack>/controls --framework iso27001 --control-id ISO27001-A.8.8-vulnerability-management
```

A passing run reports this control green; in CI, `concord gate` exits non-zero while it fails.
