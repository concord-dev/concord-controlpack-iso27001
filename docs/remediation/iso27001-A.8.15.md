# Production log groups retain ≥ 1 year and are KMS-encrypted

`ISO27001-A.8.15-logging` · framework **iso27001** · severity **high** · Technological Controls

## What this control checks

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.8.15 requires logs of activities, exceptions,
and information-security events to be produced, stored, protected,
and analysed. Concord verifies CloudWatch Logs production groups
have retention ≥ 365 days and are encrypted with a customer-managed
KMS key.

## Why it matters

ISO's floor on log retention varies by sector; 365 days is the
minimum SecOps gets useful incident-investigation value from
without paying for cold-storage archival.

## Evidence

Collected from the `aws` source (`cloudwatch_log_groups` evidence type).

## What a failure looks like

This control reports a finding when any of the following hold:

- no CloudWatch Logs evidence collected
- production log group <value> retention is <value> days (min <value>)
- production log group <value> is not KMS-encrypted
